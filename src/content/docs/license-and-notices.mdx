Each published package contains its full license and applicable third-party notices; those files are the authoritative legal texts and must travel with redistributed copies. The original notices are not replaced by this summary.

The app and Themes packages identify their own code under MIT, copyright 2026 wseffect contributors. Foldkit's vendored core retains an MIT notice copyright 2025 Devin Jameson and includes a separate snabbdom v3.6.3 MIT notice copyright 2015 Simon Friis Vindum. Foldkit and Foldkit UI identify upstream snapshots authored by Devin Jameson; the Vite plugin is also an attributed upstream snapshot.

Themes includes MIT notices for Radix Themes (copyright 2023 WorkOS), Radix Colors (copyright 2021 Radix), and radix2solid (copyright 2026 Radix2Solid contributors). The Themes package explicitly describes wseffect as an independent, unofficial project not affiliated with or endorsed by Foldkit, WorkOS, or Radix. MIT licenses do not grant rights to names, logos, or trademarks. wseffect does not claim endorsement or affiliation with upstream projects.
Every package also ships a WS Agency brand notice. Read the tagged [`WS-AGENCY-NOTICE.md` for App](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/app/WS-AGENCY-NOTICE.md), [Themes](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/themes/WS-AGENCY-NOTICE.md), [Foldkit](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/foldkit/WS-AGENCY-NOTICE.md), [Foldkit UI](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/foldkit-ui/WS-AGENCY-NOTICE.md), and [Vite plugin](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/vite-plugin/WS-AGENCY-NOTICE.md). These notices state that wseffect is independent and unofficial and is not endorsed by the named organizations; the MIT grants cover code only and grant no trademark rights to WS Agency marks.

The full legal files are published at the matching tag: [App LICENSE](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/app/LICENSE), [Themes LICENSE](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/themes/LICENSE), [Foldkit LICENSE](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/foldkit/LICENSE), [Foldkit third-party notices](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/foldkit/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md), [Foldkit UI LICENSE](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/foldkit-ui/LICENSE), and [Vite plugin LICENSE](https://github.com/wsagency/wseffect/blob/v0.1.0-rc.1/packages/vite-plugin/LICENSE). Their `UPSTREAM.md` files provide snapshot attribution and maintenance details for Foldkit, Foldkit UI, and the plugin. Use the license/notices shipped in the exact package archive when redistributing; this summary does not replace those texts.
